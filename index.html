Welcome Home

Whether you grew up at Jefferson Avenue in the 1960s, 1970s, 1980s or 1990s, you are part of a shared story. Our Sabbath experiences, music, friendships, family traditions, mentors and memories continue to connect us across generations and miles.

We are building this space to reconnect, remember, celebrate and preserve the legacy of the Jefferson Avenue family.
